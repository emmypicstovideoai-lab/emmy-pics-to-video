About Emmy AI

Turn your pictures into creative AI-generated videos.

Our Mission

Emmy Pics to Video AI is a creative platform designed to help anyone bring their photos to life. Upload a picture, describe the moment you want to see, and let AI transform it into a video.

We believe that storytelling through video should be simple, accessible, and fun for everyone — no editing skills required.

Who Built This

Emmy Pics to Video AI was created by Emmy — a passionate developer building tools that make creativity and storytelling accessible to everyone.

This website is proudly designed, developed, and maintained as an independent project. Our goal is to help people bring their memories and ideas to life through the power of AI.

How It Works

  • Upload a picture — Choose a photo from your phone or computer.
  • Describe your video — Write a prompt describing the motion, mood, or scene you want.
  • Generate — Emmy AI processes your image and prompt to produce a short video.

Current Status

Emmy AI is in testing mode. You can create an account and save video requests today, and the real AI video generator will be connected soon.

Payments are currently disabled — the site is completely free to explore.
